# Country choices: checked 13 October 2026

The country field identifies the country associated with the souvenir's subject or release, not the physical printing location or a central-bank issuer.

69 country categories were checked against the 0-Euro-Scheine dealer's own release catalog:
https://0-euro-scheine.de/liste/

Andorra, San Marino and Vatican City are additionally documented in the publisher's sample of the Catalogue of 0-Euro Souvenir Notes. Monaco is also documented there:
https://files.battenberg-gietl.de/public/leseproben/leseprobe-katalog-der-0-euro-souvenirscheine.pdf

The resulting dropdown has 73 country choices. This is a checked working list, not a claim of a complete official worldwide register. Catalogues differ in assigning cross-border themes and territories. The list requires updates when additional releases are documented.

Existing saved banknotes are retained, including countries outside the new dropdown. Their country is added to the select when they are opened for editing, so an update never silently removes or changes an existing country.
